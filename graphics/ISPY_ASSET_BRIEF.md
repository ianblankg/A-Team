# I Spy Asset Library — generation brief for Yello (Grok)

Issued by Ian via Mello, 2026-11-05. Chain Run 001 (see issue #6).

## The job

Generate the largest asset library you can for Soul Sim: furniture, small items, decorations, plants, and wildlife — an "I Spy" book's worth of things for GPT to furnish the world with. Sheet by sheet, as many as you can produce. Style and palette are locked below; within them, go wide and inventive.

## Style (locked — Ian's pick)

- **Sea of Stars intricacy:** dense, clustered pixel work — tonal clumps, layered silhouettes, irregular edges, hanging willow strands, objects with visible craft (stitched cloth, carved wood grain, stacked pages, curled vines). Nothing flat or clip-arty.
- **Hyper Light Drifter palette (the fusion base):** quiet desaturated teal/slate/bone world —
  - deep shadow `#10272f`–`#19343d`, slate `#4f6270`, stone blue-grey `#7d8b96`
  - bone `#c8c6b3`, peach-bone `#b18e7c`, pale parchment `#d8d2bd`
  - moss/fern greens desaturated: `#3f5a4a`, `#5d7a5e`
- **Reserved accents — do NOT use for decoration:** echo cyan, lamp amber, memory rose, pool teal. Those colors mean things in this game. Your library lives in the quiet base palette.
- **Nothing pure white.** `#FFFFFF` is Mello, the player character, and he must stay the brightest thing in any scene. Bone `#c8c6b3` is your lightest tone.
- Lighting stays flat (the game lights scenes itself). No baked glows, no drop shadows beyond a soft contact shade in the base palette.

## Format (so GPT can cut and place them)

- One category per sheet; items in a loose grid, generous spacing, nothing touching or overlapping.
- Flat background: solid deep shadow `#10272f` (GPT keys it out).
- Consistent scale within a sheet; furniture roughly chair-to-bookcase sized relative to a 24px-tall character. Pixel density fine and even — no mixed resolutions.
- No text, no letters, no UI elements.

## Categories (targets — exceed them if you can)

1. **House furniture & homey clutter** — chairs, stools, side tables, dressers, crates, baskets, rugs, quilts, teapots, mugs, candles (unlit shapes), jars, bottles, brooms, hats, boots, picture frames, clocks.
2. **Library / commons paper world** — book stacks, open books, scrolls, quills & inkwells, loose pages, bookmarks, spectacles, globes, maps, letter bundles, wax seals, reading lamps (unlit forms), cushions, bench cloths (desaturated slate).
3. **Workshop gear** — tools, gears, fittings, jars of parts, workbench clutter, blueprints (blank/bone), chalk diagrams (muted bone lines only, never white), stools, shelves of boxes, rope coils, lanterns (forms only).
4. **Town & hall civic pieces** — notice boards, plaques (blank), benches, planters, barrels, signposts (blank faces), stone markers, banners (muted), festival bunting (desaturated).
5. **Plants & forest life** — willow strands, fern clusters, mossy stones, mushrooms (muted), saplings, flowering shrubs (bone/peach blooms only), vines, hollow-log dressing, pool reeds, fallen leaves.
6. **Wildlife (small ambient sprites, side/top-down readable)** — birds, squirrels, rabbits, a fox, a deer, frogs, butterflies, dragonflies, fireflies (dim bone dots, no glow), a cat, an owl. Calm poses; a few variants each.
7. **Small treasures & oddments** — keys, compasses, music boxes, toys, marbles, shells, feathers, teacups, tiny statues, weather vanes — the I Spy gold.

## Delivery

Post each finished sheet (or its link) into issue #6 as your signed section (`FROM: Yello`), numbered (Sheet 01, 02, …) with its category named. The chain carries it from there: Gemini audits palette/style and catalogs; GPT slices, keys, and furnishes the world under the art standard's density rules; evidence returns to Ian through Mello.

Direction credit stays yours; the desk stays closed — this is a generation commission, not the old designer layer.
